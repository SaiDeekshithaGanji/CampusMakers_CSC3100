# Project Context: Campus Makers Marketplace

## Product

Campus Makers Marketplace is a place for student creatives,
makers, and builders to advertise their creations and grow and
manage their businesses.

Makers can:

- Create listings for their products
- Upload product images
- Share contact information
- Set product pricing
- Promote themselves and their work

## Data to Store

- Product images
- Maker contact information
- Product pricing information

## Open Questions

- Who can create a maker account?
- What information should appear on a product listing?
- How will buyers contact makers?
- Can makers edit or delete listings after publishing?
- Who reviews or removes inappropriate listings or images?
- Will the marketplace support payments, or only connect buyers
  with makers?
- What privacy rules apply to maker contact information?

## Task Prompt Pattern

Using only the evidence and confirmed decisions above:

1. Draft one user story for a named user.
2. Draft Given / When / Then acceptance criteria.
3. List assumptions separately.
4. List questions that require a manager, stakeholder, or team
   decision.
5. Do not invent privacy, payment, moderation, or
   business-policy requirements.
